VetoPoint AI brand guidelines

The visual identity of the enterprise control layer that evaluates, approves, escalates or vetoes consequential AI actions before execution.

Logo meaning

A control point where AI actions are evaluated before execution.

AI Actions
→
VetoPoint Control Point
→
Safe, Approved Action

The deep-navy V represents VetoPoint and organisational authority. The coral circle is the decisive control point, surrounded by a white boundary that signals governed review. The blue path rises through the checkpoint and becomes an arrow — an approved AI action proceeding safely. The visual language deliberately avoids generic robots, locks and shields.
