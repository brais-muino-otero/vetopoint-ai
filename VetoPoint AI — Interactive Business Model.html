02 · Early customer configuration

The same control layer answers different urgent risks.

Each segment buys governance, but the trigger, buyer, measurable outcome and expansion path differ. These panels translate the platform model into sector-specific commercial language.

Banking

Banks and payment companies face immediate, quantifiable exposure when AI agents can initiate payments, alter records or approve transactions. The first beachhead is a controlled finance or procurement workflow.

Highest urgency
Priority buyer

CRO · CISO · CCO · CFO

A cross-functional buying group owns financial control, cybersecurity, policy enforcement and accountable approval.

Land motion

€25K finance pilot

Three months, one department, up to three AI agents and three approval workflows, with measurable results and an evaluation report.

Measurable value proposition

Reduce unmanaged high-risk AI actions before execution—without sending every low-risk action to manual review.

Measure prevented unauthorised transfers, approval time, complete audit evidence and the value of protected transactions. The trade-off is deliberate friction for critical actions.

Prove risk reduction, package the learning, then scale through partners.

07 · 18-month proof points

The business model succeeds only when pilots convert.

VetoPoint’s near-term objectives are commercial and technical at the same time: build trust, prove measurable risk reduction and convert controlled deployments into contracted recurring revenue.

01

Production-ready MVP

Authentication, custom policies, secure database, approval workflows, multi-agent support and enterprise reporting.

02

Independent security testing

Validate the control layer itself through secure development, penetration testing and compliance documentation.

03

Three paid enterprise pilots

Use real workflows and policies to prove prevented risk, approval speed, audit evidence and customer trust.

04

Two pilot conversions

Convert at least two paid pilots into annual subscriptions with a clear expansion pathway.

05

Initial integrations

Connect to enterprise AI-agent platforms, identity systems, workflows and operational tools.

06

€300K–€500K contracted ARR

Establish recurring commercial proof before broad partner-led scale.
