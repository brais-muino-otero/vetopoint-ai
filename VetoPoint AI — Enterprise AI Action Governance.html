The gap
The Problem

AI adoption moved faster than AI governance did

Employees paste confidential data into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Copilot every day. Agents are starting to act on their own. Most organizations have no checkpoint between intent and execution — and no record of what happened when it mattered.

Data leaks

Confidential documents, source code, and customer data pasted into AI tools with no oversight.

Compliance violations

GDPR, HIPAA, and the EU AI Act don't pause for automation. Unlogged AI actions are unauditable actions.

Hallucinations that act

A wrong answer is a mistake. A wrong action taken by an agent is an incident with real operational cost.

Financial losses

Autonomous actions that touch payments, contracts, or trading systems carry direct financial exposure.

Reputational damage

Customers and regulators don't distinguish between "the AI did it" and "the company did it."

Shadow AI

Tools security never approved, used by teams security doesn't know about, on data security can't see.

The checkpoint
The Solution

The checkpoint AI never had

Traditional security tools protect data at rest and prompts in transit. VetoPoint AI governs what happens next. It sits in the path of every AI-generated action, understands what that action is actually trying to do, and decides — in real time — whether it runs, waits for a human, or never happens at all.

No blocking AI outright. No rubber-stamping it either. Just a governed, auditable middle ground enterprises can actually adopt.

How it works
Pipeline

Five steps. Every single action.

Every request — human or agent — passes through the same governed sequence before it ever reaches your systems.

01

Intercept

The request from an employee or AI agent is captured before it reaches company systems — no action bypasses the checkpoint.

02

Recognize intent

NLP reads what the action is actually trying to do, not just the words used to ask for it.

03

Score the risk

A dynamic model scores data sensitivity, system impact, and context — recalculated for every single request.

04

Verify policy

The scored action is checked against your organization's rules and the regulatory frameworks that apply to it.

05

Decide

Safe actions execute automatically. Risky actions stop — and wait for a human.

Safe → Execute

Cleared instantly, logged for the record.

Risky → Block / Human approval required

Held at the gate until a person signs off.

Responsible AI
Responsible AI

Responsible by design

Governing AI actions means holding ourselves to the same standard we hold your systems to. Five commitments sit underneath every decision VetoPoint AI makes.

Data privacy

GDPR-first by design. Prompts and confidential data are inspected inside your own environment and are never stored, sold, or used to train external models.

Bias & fairness

The risk-scoring model is continuously audited so that every action is evaluated on the same terms — no team, group, or individual is scored unfairly.

Transparency

Every decision — allow, hold, or block — is logged with the reason and the exact policy that triggered it, so people can always see why an action was governed the way it was.

Human oversight

VetoPoint AI never has the final word alone. Elevated-risk actions are routed to a human approver, keeping people in control of what AI is allowed to do.

Responsible use of information

Sensitive data is redacted or withheld before it can be misused, and access is scoped to the task at hand — the right information reaches the right action, and nothing more.

FAQ
FAQ

Questions security teams ask first

What exactly does VetoPoint AI govern?+

Any action initiated by an employee through an AI assistant, or by an autonomous agent acting on its own — from sending a document externally to writing to a production system.

How is this different from a DLP or prompt-filtering tool?+

DLP and prompt tools inspect content going in or out. VetoPoint AI evaluates the action itself — what it would do if executed — and can hold or block it before it happens.

Will this slow down AI adoption?+

The goal is the opposite. Low-risk actions clear automatically in milliseconds — only the actions that genuinely warrant a second look are held for a human.

What does "EU AI Act compliant" mean here?+

VetoPoint AI's risk tiers and approval workflows are mapped to the Act's obligations for high-risk AI use cases, and its audit trail is built to produce the evidence regulators ask for.

Which AI tools and agents does VetoPoint AI support?+

VetoPoint AI is designed to sit alongside ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, and custom or third-party autonomous agents.

How long does deployment take?+

We scope this with your security team based on which tools and systems VetoPoint AI needs to sit in front of. Request a demo and we'll walk through it.
